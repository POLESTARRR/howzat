How hard was it to score?

Runs per dismissal by decade, across every Test batter. This spans 1.96× — which is exactly what raw batting average ignores, and what CRI+ divides out.

The ratings

Bars show the 95% interval. A short career gives a wide bar — that is the point. Δ is how many places a batter moves versus raw batting average; positive means the era adjustment promoted them. Click any column to sort.

#PlayerInns AvgSR+ CRI+ 95% intervalΔ
Method. Innings runs modelled as Geometric with a log link; not-outs enter as right-censored observations rather than being averaged in. Player skill carries a Gaussian prior whose strength is set by empirical Bayes (λ = 1.90), so short careers shrink toward the mean. The era term is fixed from observed scoring, not fitted — estimated freely it came out anti-correlated with reality (r = −0.21), because only 12.7% of players span three decades and era is confounded with skill. Fitted by penalised MLE with analytic gradients.

CRI+ measures dominance over contemporaries, not absolute skill across eras — the same claim baseball's + metrics make, and the strongest this data supports. Data: ESPNcricinfo Statsguru.
